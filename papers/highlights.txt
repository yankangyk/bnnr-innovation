GMC: Graph Multi-view Completion for Drug Repositioning

Submitted to: Computers in Biology and Medicine

- A multi-view low-rank completion model coupling a cold-start-restricted KNN fill with scale-free rank fusion of matrix and tensor geometries.
- Achieves the highest AUPR on all four drug-disease association benchmarks under 10-fold CVa (F 0.6569, C 0.7285, CTD 0.3714, Y 0.7404).
- Significant improvements over all published baselines on CTDdataset2023 and Ydataset (p=0.002).
- Ablation study confirms each architectural component (cold-start fill, block completion, tensor completion) is load-bearing.
- The single shared hyperparameter set is robust across all four datasets without per-dataset tuning.
